# Validation and project selection

Family research guidance, October 23, 2026. The student must independently design and author the actual work. These are proposed decision criteria, not results or a submission protocol.

## A physical result comes first

| Project | Primary engineering result | What that result cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Seagrass anchors | Reduced slip/dislodgement of artificial plugs at matched measured loads, material use and embedment | Live-plant establishment, marine biodegradability, carbon storage or meadow recovery |
| Lost line retrieval | More completely recovered documented line/net material at equal time and tension limits | Safe contact forces on living coral, animal rescue, field cleanup or complete microplastic accounting |
| Cool-water refuge | More physically connected cool water maintained at matched inlet thermal and flow budgets, with adequate measured exchange and bounded backwater | Fish occupancy/survival, adequate oxygen, population recovery or permission for field installation |

For every project, compare a serious conventional design or controller on the same apparatus. A poor staple, a blind maximum-force pull or an arbitrarily weak flow rule is not an acceptable main opponent. Give competing methods equal signals, operating constraints, development data and tuning opportunities. Prediction error is secondary to the outcome after the physical intervention.

## Original data and honest ML claims

The relevant labels are outcomes of actions: anchor performance, consequences of a retrieval movement, or the cool volume following a deflector adjustment. Public observations can motivate realistic ranges and prior models; they do not label new devices. No ready-to-use large public action/outcome dataset has been verified for these three apparatus designs.

Use small, regularized models suited to the independent sample count. A deep network or unconstrained reinforcement learner is not justified merely because many sensor samples or images are recorded. Report uncertainty, calibration, failure modes and the full tradeoff between environmental outcome and cost/damage constraints. If a simple method ties, report that honestly and reconsider the ML requirement.

Split data by independent physical unit, entire entanglement episode or whole inlet sequence/day. Keep adjacent frames, repeated waves, sensor timestamps and repeated actions within their parent unit. Freeze model, comparator settings, outcome processing and exclusion rules before confirmation. Never let the controller grade its own success without an independent measurement pipeline.

The seagrass card explicitly includes a conventional selected design in physical confirmation, in addition to staples and bamboo. Its revised planning allocation is 36 training + 12 development + 48 confirmation specimens = 96 independent specimens, about 81 cyclic-rig hours at the stated protocol. Additional credible conventional designs need additional arms if distinct; lower workload cannot be obtained by silently dropping the strongest baseline. No ML sample-efficiency claim follows from shared development observations.

## Common gates before major spending

1. Required SRC authorization and the student's own risk protocol are in place before any experiment requiring approval.
2. Measurement uncertainty is smaller than a meaningful effect; the pilot is reproducible and resolves a real engineering tradeoff.
3. The complete purchase list, including calibration, independent units, failures, cameras, shipping/tax, entry and display, remains below $1,200.
4. All critical tests can run in contained indoor conditions without unconfirmed marine access, specialist machines or live protected species.
5. The best conventional method leaves an actual opportunity for learned intervention. If it does not, stop the AI claim rather than adding model complexity.
6. There is enough time after approval for development, frozen confirmation, repairs and student-written analysis. The fair date is not a reason to run the entire experiment at the last minute.

The cards' replicate counts are planning allocations, not statistical power calculations. Use pilot variance, practical effect size and attainable time to set final replication. Report individual-unit results and uncertainty even when a significance test is inconclusive.

## Ranking and remaining uncertainty

**First overall: cool-water refuges.** The physical thermal endpoint is accessible, indoor testing is possible in winter and no biology is needed to establish the bounded hydraulic/thermal result. Risks are coarse temperature mapping, sensor lag, thermal resets and a conventional passive design that makes adaptive control unnecessary.

**First marine: seagrass retention.** The restoration bottleneck and mechanical experiment are understandable. Strong cheap existing anchors, student prior art and a long endurance-testing workload make its novelty and schedule less secure. It is a mechanical pilot, not a proven marine restoration intervention.

**Alternative: lost-line disentanglement.** Actual material can be recovered and weighed, but limited robot motions may not free representative tangles. Most real operations involve cutting, biofouling and professional divers. Whether the restricted subtask is useful to practitioners remains unverified.

None is automatically an ISEF-caliber project. A defensible competition result would require a meaningful, reproducible physical improvement over strong baselines, student ownership, careful prior-art analysis and appropriately bounded environmental claims.
