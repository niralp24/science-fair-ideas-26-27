## What would make a project worth pursuing

The student must physically improve the measured condition or recovery outcome, and the learned method must beat a competent simple alternative. Accurate detection, a forecast, or a high training score does not satisfy that requirement. Each idea has explicit stop criteria. Original physical intervention data are essential; there is no verified public dataset that labels the action consequences of these particular devices.

Cool-water refuges remain the strongest overall candidate for a limited pilot in this repository. Among the new additions, oil recovery is the most direct cleanup experiment; native-seed treatment offers a living-plant endpoint but has the tightest biological timing; reef rubble is a reserve. Seagrass retention remains another marine restoration option. No candidate is a confirmed novel invention. The possible contribution is a narrowly defined, independently tested improvement over established engineering. Competition advancement and publication cannot be predicted from an idea description.
